After spending a decade in the tech industry, I've come to realize that the most valuable skill isn't coding—it's communication. 🧵

Let me share three lessons that transformed my career trajectory.

First, listening is a superpower. Early in my career, I would rush to propose solutions before fully understanding the problem. This approach led to countless reworks and missed opportunities. When I started asking more questions and truly absorbing feedback, my work improved dramatically.

Second, writing clearly is thinking clearly. Documentation, emails, proposals—these aren't bureaucratic overhead. They're tools that force you to organize your thoughts and expose gaps in your reasoning.

Third, empathy drives better products. Understanding your users isn't just about analytics dashboards. It's about having real conversations and feeling their frustrations firsthand.

The tech industry rewards those who can bridge the gap between technical complexity and human understanding.
