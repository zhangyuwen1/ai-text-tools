Q2 is done. Revenue hit $4.2 million. That's up 12% from last year, and 8% better than what we'd projected internally. Most of it came from enterprise — 62% of total revenue, honestly.

Retention stayed at 94%. Our customer success programs are working, and that number shows it.

Operating expenses went up 5%. That's mostly product development and marketing — we're spending on purpose, and we expect it to pay off in the quarters ahead. Cash flow from operations stayed positive, at $680,000.

So where does that leave us? The leadership team is cautiously optimistic about hitting our annual targets, even with the macroeconomic uncertainty that keeps throwing headwinds our way. Not great, but manageable. And look, a 12% jump with 94% retention is a solid place to be.

We'll keep watching the numbers.
