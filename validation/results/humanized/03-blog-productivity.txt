Productivity isn't a buzzword anymore. It's how you keep your job. Folks everywhere are drowning in tasks. Everything feels urgent. Nothing gets done.

Here's the thing, though. You can fix this. A few proven tricks will do it.

Start with the Eisenhower Matrix. It's old, it's simple, and it works. Sort your tasks by what actually matters and what just feels urgent. Those aren't the same thing.

Then try the Pomodoro Technique. Work 25 minutes. Take a short break. Repeat. People who use it say their output goes up and their head gets clearer.

Cut the noise too. Notifications, pointless meetings, all of it. Kill them. That's how you slip into a flow state, where the real work happens.

Honestly, productivity isn't about doing more things. It's about doing the right things. That's the whole game.
