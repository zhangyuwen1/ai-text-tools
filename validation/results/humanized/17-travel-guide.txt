Kyoto was Japan's capital for over a thousand years. You can feel that.

The city has more than 1,600 Buddhist temples and 400 Shinto shrines. Weeks of exploring won't cover it all. Fushimi Inari Shrine is the big one. Thousands of vermilion torii gates. It's one of the most photographed spots in Japan. And look, Gion is worth a wander too. If you're lucky you'll spot geiko and maiko heading to evening engagements.

Spring brings cherry blossoms. The whole city turns pink and white. People fly in from everywhere. Not a bad time to visit. Fall is just as good, honestly. The maple trees go bright red and orange. Thing is, everyone knows this. Peak seasons get packed. Go early in the morning if you can. Architecture, food, quiet gardens — Kyoto's got all of it.
