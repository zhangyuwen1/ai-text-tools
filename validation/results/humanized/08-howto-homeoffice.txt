Your home office setup matters. A lot. Good ergonomics keep you productive and keep your body from falling apart.

Start with the chair. Get one with real lumbar support that helps you sit up straight all day. Then think about your monitor. It should sit at eye level, about an arm's length away. That cuts the strain on your neck and your eyes.

Elbows at 90 degrees. That's the target for your keyboard and mouse height. And look, lighting counts too. Natural light is best. No window? Mix ambient and task lighting so you're not squinting or fighting glare all afternoon.

Take breaks. The 20-20-20 rule is simple: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. It does a lot to ease digital eye strain. Honestly, it's the easiest fix on this list.

One more thing people skip. Draw a line between where you work and where you live. Not great to blur that. A healthy work-life balance depends on it, and so does your sanity.

Do these things and your workspace works for you. Comfort and efficiency, both.
