Ten years in tech taught me something. Coding isn't the top skill. Communication is. 🧵

Three lessons changed my career.

First: listening. Sounds soft. It's not. Early on, I'd jump straight to fixes before I really got the problem. Tons of rework followed. Missed chances too. Then I started asking more questions and actually soaking up feedback. My work got way better. Dramatically.

Second, clear writing means clear thinking. Docs, emails, proposals. Not paperwork. They're tools. They force you to sort out your thoughts and show you where your logic has holes.

Third, empathy builds better products. Analytics dashboards only tell you so much. You've got to talk to real users. Feel their pain yourself.

And look, the industry pays well for people who can connect technical mess to human understanding.
